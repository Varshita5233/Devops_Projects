Your app "{{ .Release.Name }}" is deployed!

Get the public URL (may take 1-2 min for the load balancer to be ready):

  kubectl get svc {{ .Release.Name }}-myapp -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} \
    -o jsonpath='{.status.loadBalancer.ingress[0].hostname}'

Then open http://<that-hostname> in your browser.

Check the pods:
  kubectl get pods -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
